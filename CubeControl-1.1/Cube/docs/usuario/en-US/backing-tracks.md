# Backing Tracks

Find tracks on YouTube, listen to a preview and download MP3 audio.

## Find tracks

Enter at least three characters. Search starts automatically after a brief pause. The button beside the search adds “backing track” to the query; turn it off to search only the text you entered.

## Preview audio

Choose an audio output and click Preview on a result card to listen to up to 30 seconds. Adjust Volume or Mute without changing the downloaded file. Preview on hover is remembered between launches.

Click Stop preview or press Escape to stop. Leaving the page, changing outputs or starting IR audition also stops the preview. Starting a YouTube preview stops IR audition.

## Download MP3

Click Download MP3. Choose a folder on your first download; use Change folder to select another destination. The folder and completed downloads are remembered. The card shows progress and lets you cancel. Repeated downloads get another filename to preserve existing files.

Audio is saved in the selected folder for use in another application. Existing audio and old projects remain on disk.

## Troubleshooting

Search, preview and download require internet access. If the folder is unavailable, select another with Change folder. If audio tools are missing, reinstall the complete application package. Use your own or authorized content.
