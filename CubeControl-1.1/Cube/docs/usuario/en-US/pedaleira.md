# Pedalboard and effects

## Navigate modules
The visual order is Amp → Chorus → Phaser → Delay → Reverb → Cabinet. Click a card to open its editor; its switch performs a separate action. The wheel over the chain navigates between modules.

## Understand the controls
- Amp Tone is not a general amplifier bypass.
- Chorus and Phaser share a modulation parameter and cannot be active simultaneously.
- Adjustments to a disabled effect may remain local until it is enabled.
- Delay time is a pedal index with no verified conversion to milliseconds. Do not interpret every number as a percentage, Hz or dB.
- Images and commercial names are presentation references, not certification of the contents of a slot.

## Choose an amp and cabinet
Click the image to open the selector. Apply or double-click confirms; cancel preserves the previous selection. Where a gallery is available, the wheel over the image can also change equipment.

See [IRs and cabinets](irs.md) or [Effect creators](criadores-efeitos.md) for advanced internal adjustments. Changing a picture does not change the sound.
