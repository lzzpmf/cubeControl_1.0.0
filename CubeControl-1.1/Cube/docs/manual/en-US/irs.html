    Quick workflow

    Before you start. Have a compatible impulse-response WAV and a backup of the slot you intend to replace.

    1. Open Manage IRs and select a file in the individual tab.
    2. With the pedal connected, check the preview state and listen.
    3. End the preview to restore, or use the write action and check its destination.

    When finished. Previewing and replacing a slot have different outcomes; check the message before continuing.

    Choose a cabinet

    The pedal has eight IR slots. Their contents may have been replaced: a slot number does not identify a cabinet. The included library works offline.

    Shared library

    Manage IRs has Individual analysis and preview and IR packs (8 slots) tabs sharing the same library, search and selection. The individual tab opens initially; the selected tab is retained during the session. In Packs, selecting a file only makes it available for slot assembly. Switching tabs does not apply IRs or discard the draft; an active preview remains on the pedal. Return to the individual tab to end it.

    Import and try an IR

    Choose an impulse-response WAV in the interface and check the reported compatibility. An IR WAV is neither an amp file nor a Backing Tracks song. IR preview changes pedal RAM without replacing any of the eight stored slots. Choosing an external WAV or selecting an IR in the individual library starts preview automatically when the pedal is connected and the session is not blocked; without a connection, it only prepares the local file. The previous state remains in memory for this app session; use End preview before closing.

    Importing to a slot and writing are device operations. Export or preserve the backup of the previous contents before replacement. Read the messages and the destination slot in the confirmation.

    Audition with recorded guitar

    Select one of the five guitar recordings and choose the pedal’s USB audio output. An operational MIDI connection is required, but loading an IR is optional. Without an IR preview you hear the current preset. Audition follows changes applied to the effect chain, including Amp Creator previews; edits that remain only on the computer do not affect the sound. An interrupted IR preview must be confirmed or restored before starting. The computer plays the raw WAV; the pedal processes it with its current effects. Outputs come from Windows: choosing another output does not apply the IR in software. Volume starts at 30% and looping is enabled.

    Changing the IR pauses and mutes audition during transfer; playback resumes at the same position only after confirmation. Pause keeps the position; Stop returns to the beginning. Changing the recording during playback restarts with the new recording. Playback continues when navigating to the pedalboard or Amp Creator, with compact pause and stop controls. Ending IR preview pauses during restoration and resumes the restored preset after confirmation. Changing output, disconnecting or closing the app stops playback. Backing Tracks, YouTube previews and IR audition do not play simultaneously.

    Build and apply packs

    The catalog includes 34 predefined packs, grouped by brand, style and guitar, with eight slots and an initial distance of 80%. Descriptions suggest uses; they do not simulate guitar models or imply listening validation. Personal packs appear under My packs.

    In IR packs, choose any pack and edit it directly. The first change to a predefined pack creates an independent draft and shows the yellow Creating pack notice; the original stays intact. Remove all eight IRs to start empty. Drag a library file onto the destination card. Dragging between cards swaps their IRs and distances. The trash icon appears on hover or keyboard focus; Delete also removes an IR. The context menu provides keyboard alternatives. Empty slots preserve the pedal's current contents. Building and searching do not start a preview or write to the device.

    Adjust Volume directly with each card’s knob, from 0 to 99% (distance in the protocol). Its value appears in the tooltip and is stored when saving the pack. Click Apply to send populated slots: only an action confirmation appears, with OK or Exit. The app compares destinations, keeps a backup and skips identical contents. Empty slots remain unchanged; fix or remove missing references before applying.

    The Apply and Restore original IRs actions appear below the grid. Save pack asks for a name and description in a popup and stores your draft for reuse; saving is optional before applying. Saving a draft based on a predefined pack creates a personal pack; saving changes to a personal pack updates it. Discarding restores the source pack. When switching a changed pack, choose Save, Discard, or Cancel.

    Export creates a .cubeirpack.zip containing the WAVs, which can be imported on another computer. Import opens a draft for review without automatically saving or applying it. Imported WAVs are stored in user data and also appear in the individual library. Older .cubeirpack.json files remain supported but require their referenced WAVs. Missing references are shown on cards and cannot be selected for application.

    Binary BFI/BFIN packs are available through the separate Restore original IRs action when original resources are available. See Backups and recovery.

    Limits and partial application

    Individual WAVs and WAVs in a portable pack are limited to 32 MiB per file. The pack validates all WAV hashes and formats before copying them to the imported library; changed or invalid files are rejected. Importing a ZIP can already copy WAVs to user data before you save the draft.

    Applying a pack writes one slot at a time. If it fails, the message lists completed slots; it does not automatically undo slots already written. Check the result before retrying. Automatic copies of previous contents remain only in session memory; export backups you need to keep.

    Random cabinet IR

    In the effect chain, under Cabinet, Randomize and apply chooses from the entire library, including imports and ignoring search filters, with an integer volume from 66% to 85% (the protocol calls this parameter distance). The destination is the current preset cabinet, not the separately selected slot on the IR page. It writes directly without another confirmation and applies in RAM for immediate use; it does not save the preset or change the IR section on/off state. Repeats are possible.

    A working connection, loaded bank, valid slot and available library are required. The file is validated before writing. If writing succeeds but immediate application fails, the message distinguishes the two: the slot has already been replaced. Previous contents are retained only in session memory.

    Associate with an amp

    Associate cabinet applies the configured amp association to the edited preset. Associations and the automatic-association option remain in the current session only; reopening starts with automatic association disabled and requires configuring associations again. Saving associations does not send commands to the pedal. When enabled, it attempts to apply the association to the current amp and subsequent choices. Without a valid association, the cabinet is preserved.

    Identity and pictures

    Manufacturer names and photos help selection but do not prove that an IR exactly reproduces that equipment. Visual customization does not change audio.

    See Backups and recovery.

    Overdriven.fr collections

    The Overdriven.fr library contains 13 collections and 682 IRs. Search matches every typed term, case-insensitively, against file paths, names and metadata. Clear the text to restore the full library. There are no separate collection, microphone or distance selectors. Searching does not send audio to the pedal; selecting an IR in the individual library can start preview.

    Files with metadata follow brand → speaker configuration → model and speaker → capture. Overdriven.fr identifies the source, not a cabinet brand. Files without metadata follow their folders; imported files appear under Imported IRs. Expanding one brand collapses the others. Selection details show original format, source, identification, evidence, position, variants and filename. Microphone models marked “inspired by” come from the provider documentation. Undocumented codes are preserved without assumed equivalents.

    In the provider's visual reference, P00 is the dust cap center, P05 is on the cap, P09 near its edge, P10 at its edge, P20 on the cone and P30 near the outer edge. Other codes, such as P12 and P25, have no exact documented location in that image. The original photo is not bundled with the app.

    Filename distances are approximate millimeters. They describe the capture and do not change the app's 0–99 Distance control. Suffixes such as LC45, EQ, P0, CUT1 and UA610 identify variants, without triggering processing in the app. SP1-PREVIEW marks preview files, each identified by its own microphone and original name.

    All files are mono at 44.1 kHz: 82 GOV IRs are PCM 16-bit/50 ms (their readme states float 32-bit); the rest are PCM 24-bit/100 ms. The app converts to 48 kHz and retains 512 samples, approximately 10.7 ms, without gain normalization. It applies no automatic cuts, EQ, gain or phase correction. The provider's filter and adjustment recommendations are listening suggestions.
